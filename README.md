# ViTrox Campus Sim

A Sims-style 3D campus simulation: run ViTrox College on its new Batu Kawan site and grow it from a College into a University College, then a University.

**Languages:** English · Bahasa Melayu · 中文

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs an internet connection the first time, to load three.js and Google Fonts from public CDNs.

## Features

- 3D campus you can orbit and zoom, with day and night, moving sun and shadows.
- Students and staff with needs (Hunger, Energy, Fun, Social, Learning), moods, thoughts and conversations.
- Click a building to see inside it, or follow any student or lecturer up close.
- Build teaching blocks, labs, a Smart Factory Lab, hostel, library, research centre, sports courts, running track, bike station and more.
- Hire academics, sponsor PhDs, launch programmes, manage finances and policies.
- Industry-driven education: sign partner companies, build a WBL Studio and balance placement places, supervisors and eligible students.
- Student success: pass rates, students at risk, dropouts and graduation rates, shaped by class sizes, PhD share, facilities, academic support and entry standard.
- Accreditation and quality assurance: curriculum reviews, industry advisory panels, CQI plans and periodic MQA audits that can add conditions or suspend intake.
- AI Advisor: reads your numbers, ranks the biggest problems and lays out options with pros, risks and estimated impact.
- Scenario mode, reports with trend charts and a yearly decision review, and a comic-book art style.
- ViTrox HQ (Campus 2.0) next door: students intern there under Work-Based Learning.
- Koi pond, campus events, and the upgrade path to University College and University.

## Disclaimer

Unofficial fan-made simulation. Upgrade criteria, fees and numbers are simplified game rules loosely inspired by Malaysia's private higher-education framework. They are not official MOHE or MQA requirements. All people in the game are fictional, and partner companies other than ViTrox are fictional. The AI Advisor is part of the game: its suggestions come from the game's own rules.
